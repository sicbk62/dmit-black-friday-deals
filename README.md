## What DMIT actually did during Black Friday, 2020 to 2023

Knowing the shape of the old promotions is useful because it tells you what to look for if a sale does return, and what you are giving up by not waiting.

**Pro series, buy two get one free.** Buy any two Pro products (LAX.Pro, HKG.Pro and TYO.Pro all counted) and DMIT threw in a third Pro plan. When the two paid plans differed in price, the free one was the cheaper of the pair. The free plan was free for the first billing cycle only; renewals were charged normally. This was the headline offer most years.

**Spend-based account credit.** New orders on the Tier 1 and Eyeball series earned credit against cumulative spend, typically $10 back at $100, $50 at $500 and $250 at $1,000. The 2023 round raised the top tier to $250.

**The 2023 high-percentage round.** New purchases of SJC.T1.STARTER or above returned 50% of the order as account credit after three months; HKG.T1.STARTER or above returned 70%. Both required opening a support ticket to register, and both came with the three-month waiting period. This was the most aggressive Black Friday DMIT has run, and nothing since has matched it.

**Route Booster rebate.** Ticking the Route Booster IPv4 option during that period returned 50% of the option fee as credit.

The credit rules matter more than the percentages. Account credit cannot be withdrawn, and if you request a refund DMIT deducts the credited amount from it. A "$100 back" headline is a coupon for your next DMIT purchase, not money.

## What the discounts are actually worth in dollars

WEE at $36.90 a year works out to $3.08 a month. Pro TINY at $10.90 a month costs $130.80 across twelve monthly renewals. Pro STARTER at $34.90 a month is $418.80 a year. The gap between WEE and TINY is not a pricing trick: WEE halves the RAM and drops to a 1Gbps port, while TINY sits on the Premium network profile. If your workload is a personal proxy, a DNS resolver or a backup node, the extra $94 a year buys you very little. If you are serving a site to mainland China users, the network profile is the entire point and the cheap plan is the wrong place to save.

Credit rebates deserve the same arithmetic. A $50 credit on a $100 order is not half price. You hand over $100 and receive $150 of service, which is a 33% effective discount and only if you buy again. The 2023 Hong Kong round at 70% back works out to roughly 41%, after a three-month wait and a support ticket. Compare that with a code that takes 30% off the price today, repeats on every renewal, and requires no follow-up.

## Restrictions that decide the purchase more than the discount does

- **The Lite annual code skips the cheapest plan.** TINY is explicitly excluded, so if TINY is what you need, waiting for a Lite sale gets you nothing.
- **The HKG/TYO code skips WEE and existing services.** New orders only, quarterly or longer.
- **Free traffic resets are capped and time-limited.** Each reset restores 20% of the base monthly allowance, a product can be reset free twice, and the programme runs to 31 December 2026. That is up to 40% extra traffic in total, not unlimited top-ups. The credits are already sitting in eligible accounts and are applied from the control panel.
- **The AS3 platform comes with DMIT's own warning.** The company has said the LAX AS3 series is still being optimised and may show degraded disk performance and a lower SLA than its mature platforms, and recommends stable buyers pick a mature location. AS3 Pro has been restocked twice since late September; Eyeball stock on AS3 has been tight.
- **Support is under load.** The 27 October notice about longer wait times and self-service preference is worth reading before you buy a plan you expect to be hand-held through.
- **The network has incidents.** LAX AS9929 packet loss on 20 October and an IP transit maintenance window through 3 November are routine reminders that CN2 GIA is a routing choice, not an uptime guarantee.

## Questions buyers keep asking

**Will DMIT run a Black Friday sale this year?** Nothing has been announced as of early November 2026, and 2025 was skipped in favour of a December promotion. Watch dmitnews; if a teaser appears, it will appear there first. Do not plan around a specific date, because none has been published.

**Do the codes stack with a Black Friday promotion?** There is no documentation of code stacking, and DMIT's historical Black Friday offers were structured as separate programmes (buy-two-get-one, spend-based credit, per-plan rebates) rather than as coupon multipliers. Assume one mechanism at a time until the cart says otherwise.

**Can account credit be withdrawn?** No. It is spendable only at DMIT, and refunds have the credited amount deducted.

**Is WEE enough to run a site?** For a low-traffic static site, a proxy or a monitoring node, yes. For anything with database writes, container builds or concurrent PHP workers, 1GB of RAM and a 1Gbps port will frustrate you within a month, and TINY at $10.90 a month is the honest step up.
